# Ghosts of the Void — NASA Archival Photography Documentation

This directory contains authentic, verified historical space exploration photographs from official NASA mission archives (primarily NASA Johnson Space Center and NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory).

## Lunar Relic Photographs

### 1. Apollo 11 Lunar Module Descent Stage
- **Local Asset:** `public/assets/relics/apollo-11-descent-stage.jpg`
- **NASA Catalog ID:** `as11-40-5927` (JSC)
- **Alt Text:** `NASA archival photograph of the Apollo 11 Eagle lunar module descent stage on the Moon.`
- **Credit:** NASA / Neil Armstrong (AS11-40-5927)
- **Official Source:** [NASA Image and Video Library - as11-40-5927](https://images.nasa.gov/details/as11-40-5927)
- **Direct NASA Media URL:** `https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/as11-40-5927/as11-40-5927~large.jpg`
- **Historical Subject:** Apollo 11 Lunar Module *Eagle* descent stage resting at Tranquility Base on July 20, 1969. Astronaut Edwin "Buzz" Aldrin is photographed by Neil Armstrong removing the Early Apollo Scientific Experiments Package (EASEP) from the descent stage equipment bay.

### 2. Surveyor 3
- **Local Asset:** `public/assets/relics/surveyor-3.jpg`
- **NASA Catalog ID:** `AS12-48-7133` (JSC)
- **Alt Text:** `NASA archival photograph of the Surveyor 3 lunar spacecraft.`
- **Credit:** NASA / Apollo 12 Crew (AS12-48-7133)
- **Official Source:** [NASA Image and Video Library - AS12-48-7133](https://images.nasa.gov/details/AS12-48-7133)
- **Direct NASA Media URL:** `https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/AS12-48-7133/AS12-48-7133~large.jpg`
- **Historical Subject:** Apollo 12 Commander Pete Conrad inspecting Surveyor 3 inside Surveyor Crater, Ocean of Storms, on November 20, 1969. The Apollo 12 Lunar Module *Intrepid* is visible on the crater rim in the background ~160 meters away.

### 3. Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicle
- **Local Asset:** `public/assets/relics/apollo-lunar-roving-vehicle.jpg`
- **NASA Catalog ID:** `as15-88-11901` (JSC)
- **Alt Text:** `NASA archival photograph of the Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicle on the lunar surface.`
- **Credit:** NASA / Apollo 15 Crew (AS15-88-11901)
- **Official Source:** [NASA Image and Video Library - as15-88-11901](https://images.nasa.gov/details/as15-88-11901)
- **Direct NASA Media URL:** `https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/as15-88-11901/as15-88-11901~large.jpg`
- **Historical Subject:** The Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV-001) photographed during EVA-3 at the Hadley-Apennine landing site. All four woven piano-wire mesh wheels, chassis, crew seats, high-gain parabolic umbrella dish antenna, and television camera are clearly displayed against Mount Hadley in the background.
